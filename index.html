About me

I'm Marwa — I've been throwing pots out of a small studio in Cairo for the past few years, and every piece you see here started as a lump of clay on my wheel. I make small batches, glaze everything myself, and no two pieces come out quite the same — that's kind of the point. If you want something made specifically for your space, I take on a few commissions each month too.
