# Client Brief #01 — Level: Entry

**From:** Marwa Adel, owner of "Adel & Co. Ceramics" — a small handmade pottery studio in Cairo
*(Note: this is a simulated brief written to imitate a real client engagement — not an actual business.)*

**Project:** One-page studio site

**Background:** Marwa hand-throws ceramics and sells through Instagram DMs right now, which she's tired of managing. She wants a simple website she can send people to instead — something that looks credible enough that people trust her enough to message her about a commission or visit her studio. She is not technical at all and has no existing brand assets beyond an Instagram feed.

**What was needed:**
- A single-page site with: a hero section (studio name + short tagline), an "About" section (2-3 sentences about her and her process), a gallery section showing a grid of her work (placeholder images, real photos to follow later), and a contact section with email, phone number, and Instagram handle
- Needs to look good on a phone — most people find her through Instagram and click through on mobile
- Should feel "warm and handmade," not corporate or techy — her exact words: "not like a SaaS startup page"
- A simple nav or scroll indicator so people know there's more below the fold

**Constraints:** No backend, no contact form submission — just display contact info/links. No CMS. Static site only.

**What done looks like:** A single HTML file (CSS inline or separate) that could theoretically be handed to a hosting service and go live. Should look intentional and finished, not like a placeholder template. Responsive from mobile up to desktop.

**Skills this tests:** Semantic HTML structure, CSS Flexbox/Grid layout, responsive design (mobile-first), visual/typographic sense, translating a vague aesthetic direction ("warm and handmade") into concrete design decisions.
